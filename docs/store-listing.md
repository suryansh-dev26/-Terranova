## Full description (4000 chars max)

**Your city is the game board. Go claim it.**

RunRealm turns every run into a land grab. Close a loop around your block, your park, your campus — and that ground becomes YOUR territory on a live shared map. Until someone runs it away from you.

**🏃 RUN**
Start a run and RunRealm traces your route live on a beautiful 3D map. Distance, time, and position update in real time, with smart GPS filtering that keeps your route clean even when the signal isn't.

**🎯 CLOSE THE LOOP**
No buttons to fiddle with mid-run. The moment your route loops back to where you started, RunRealm detects it automatically, ends the run, and captures everything inside your loop. The on-screen guide shows exactly how far you are from closing it.

**🏴 CAPTURE TERRITORY**
Your loops become territory — real polygons on a map every player shares. Run overlapping loops and they merge into one growing empire.

**⚔️ INVADE YOUR RIVALS**
Territory isn't safe. Run through another player's land and you'll cut a piece out of it. Surround it completely and you take it all — they'll see the invasion on their map, you get their streets. Defend what's yours by running it again.

**🏆 CLIMB THE LEADERBOARD**
Every square metre counts. Compete with runners everywhere for the most captured area, and keep your daily streak alive — miss a day and it resets.

**📊 TRACK YOUR EMPIRE**
Your profile shows lifetime distance, total area owned, best single run, current streak, and your recent history — all your conquests in one place.

**🌙 MADE TO BE USED**
Clean, modern interface with full dark mode. Standard, satellite, and terrain map styles. No clutter, no gimmicks.

**🔒 NO ACCOUNT. NO ADS. NO TRACKING.**
No sign-up, no email, no ads, no analytics SDKs. You get an anonymous runner identity, your location is used only while you're recording a run, and a single button deletes all your data forever. That's it.

Lace up. Someone is eyeing your street right now.
